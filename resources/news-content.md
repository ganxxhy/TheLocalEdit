# The Local Edit: supplied content

## Indie game festival brings local developers together

**Gaming · 12 September 2026**

Independent developers from across the region gathered at the Civic Centre this weekend to showcase new games, exchange ideas and meet local players. The programme included playable demos, short talks and a small competition for student projects.

## Retro arcade opens in the city centre

**Culture · 10 September 2026**

A new retro arcade has opened on Market Street with more than thirty classic machines from the 1980s and 1990s. The venue also hosts weekly tournaments and introductory sessions for visitors interested in the history of video games.

## Students create video games in weekend game jam

**Education · 8 September 2026**

Students from local schools and universities spent the weekend designing and developing small video games as part of a 48-hour game jam. Teams worked on programming, art, sound and game design before presenting their projects to the public on Sunday afternoon.

## Esports tournament fills municipal sports hall

**Esports · 5 September 2026**

More than two hundred players and spectators attended the city's first municipal esports tournament on Saturday. Local teams competed throughout the day, while visitors could try different games and attend workshops about competitive gaming and streaming.
